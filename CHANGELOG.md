# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are listed here, newest first.

## 0.9 - Main Menu
- New main menu: Start, Continue, Saves, Settings, Controls, Changelog, Credits and Quit.
- Three save slots with a summary of each (location, abilities, charms, bosses, exploration, play time).
- Settings for difficulty, screen shake, vignette, FPS counter and fullscreen, remembered between sessions.
- Pause menu on Esc.

## 0.8 - The Seraph
- New final boss: a colossal angelic being that floats in the background.
- Lightning bolts are telegraphed with warning beams before they strike.
- Giant hands slam down; striking the sigil on a resting hand damages the Seraph.
- Three phases adding halo rings of light and sweeping judgement bolts.

## 0.7 - Charms
- Ten equippable charms and a notch system. Bosses grant extra notches.
- Enemies grow tougher and hit harder in deeper regions.

## 0.6 - The Abyss
- After the Warden, a gate opens into about 60 generated rooms across four regions, each ending in a guardian boss.
- Save and load, plus a world map that scales to the full world.

## 0.5 - Polish and fixes
- Fixed Focus not working: the ground check flickered while standing still.
- Walking uses a two-bone leg animation.
- Jumps use squash and stretch with a little float at the top.

## 0.4 - Expansion
- World grew to 14 connected rooms with doors, benches and a map.
- NPCs, shops and upgrades for Geo.
- New monsters: spitters, hoppers, armored beetles, bats and ghosts.
- New abilities: dash, double jump and wall cling.
- Bosses: the Husk Brute, the Crystal Weaver and the Warden.

## 0.3 - Look and feel
- Flowing cape, textured stone and moss, parallax cave background, glowing props, lanterns and a vignette.
- More lifelike monster art.

## 0.2 - Bug fixes
- Fixed a crash on starting a game and a key lookup crash.

## 0.1 - First playable
- Nail combat with pogo, dash, Soul and Focus healing, Geo, benches, death shades, three rooms and the first boss.
